# Grown shared-tail topology

The generated overlap catalog chose complete pair/trio leg plans, then moved and rotated them. `PAIR_PATTERNS`, `TRIO_PATTERNS`, `walkOverlapMember` and their catalog-only helpers are removed. Campaign and previews use the same runtime generator; historical catalog scripts do not supply production levels.

`constructOverlap` grows a two-to-six-cell common spine through the actual surface topology, samples each member's peel point independently, then grows a three-to-ten-step branch with sampled runs and turns. Members may peel at different points and cross seams. Actual validation and movement reject shared heads, non-prefix intersections, intersecting future routes and failed group exits. Earlier reservations constrain both bodies and tracks. Construction is bounded and has no stamped fallback; independent `:overlap-topology-v1` construction leaves the mechanic presence plans intact.

The group remains one dependency-fill node. Its lane union receives ordinary bodies as predecessors, and the assembled certificate removes all members together. The new regression checks the earliest colliding member's trace, because the aggregate move's clicked-member blocker does not necessarily describe that collision. It proves one life deduction, every member red and present, and every member rewound when an ordinary body blocks the group.

## Scope and acceptance boundary

Generated levels from 16 onward reroll their overlap-dependent topology. Earlier and authored levels must retain exact fingerprints. Generator version 11 and presence-plan streams remain unchanged. Authored level 15 remains a unique fixed lesson whose IDs, guided interaction and help image require coordinated review; it no longer supplies generated groups. That is an explicit review boundary, not an exemption from the user's design request.

CPU engine, solver and text layouts are evidence of movement and topology. This cloud has no verified real-GPU browser acceptance; renderer, picking, animation and help captures are not UI-validated here. Fragile, lock, mirror, leap, their attached blockers and the static directional motif remain in the overall audit and require subsequent batches.

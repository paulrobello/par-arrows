Replacing the double with a single deadlocks the circuit. The native bodies vary between three- and four-arrow circuits; there are no A/B coordinate patterns, transformed gadgets or stamped fallbacks.

Ordinary fill bodies can block that lane and leave before the endpoint move through the assembled graph certificate. Both endpoint validation, density/depth gates, arrow budgets, shape caps, body-length/bending floors and existing placement/blocker-share floors remain authoritative. Changed saves refresh through the existing fingerprint check; generator version 11 remains unchanged.

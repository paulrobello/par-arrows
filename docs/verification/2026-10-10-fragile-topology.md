# Grown fragile ordering topology

The production fragile core previously copied level 45's crosser and bent six-cell double around a crack, then rotated and translated that assembly. FRAGILE_PATTERN and its placement implementation are removed. Campaign, preview and worker generation share the changed runtime constructor.

constructFragile grows a five-to-ten-cell double, samples a crack along its head route and constructs an independent crosser approach. A bounded surface connector links the crosser's body to a sampled double-tail contact. Zero to two grown dependencies block the tail route or an earlier dependency. Actual movement proves the competing orders: the crosser initially exits; the double's tail is blocked; choosing its head first collapses the crack and makes the crosser fall. On ground that wrong order exits safely. The safe certificate collapses the crack and selects the double's tail. Every safe choice remains free of soft-locks. A stripped deadlock test would be inappropriate because fragile terrain only restricts safe choices.

Both endpoints, all relevant heading states, the crack and earlier body/route reservations constrain construction. Exhaustion omits this optional structure; there is no stamped fallback. The independent fragile-topology-v1 stream preserves the original frequency and presence plan. Generator version 11 remains unchanged.

Attached fragile blockers now grow three-to-eight-cell bodies around sampled lane contacts with one-to-four-cell stems. They use the certificate's actual endpoint when the owner is double-ended. They retain the original presence curve, exact arrow accounting, shape accounting and mechanic-cell protections.

The new geometry exposed an existing reservation mismatch: dependency nodes and the certificate used the double's tail, but the lane-release pass released only its default head reach. Empty tail lanes remained fenced against ordinary fill. Releasing both endpoints resolves the natural-blocker coverage failure through the existing graph, without a new fill nudge or a relaxed floor. The shared bounded surface connector was extracted from parking without changing its RNG or traversal behavior.
